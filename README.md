This program is a Shoot’em up made in C++ on Visual Studio 2026.

You can move your ship with “zqsd” and shoot with “space bar”.

<img width="131" height="147" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1a7327e8-be30-443f-81ee-62af85daa080" />

The program starts on the menu and you can start or quit the game by clicking on the buttons.

<img width="302" height="284" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7d3af4a1-691d-4161-8031-f52457ee70fe" />

Your goal is to destroy as many enemies as possible.

<img width="44" height="52" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1018e6d0-fc80-417b-adb9-f4e2f523528b" />

Each kill gives you 100 points.

The game contains 2 different levels with a duration of 2 minutes.

You have 3hp to complete all the levels, and each collision with an enemy will make you lose one hp.

You can see your hp to the down left of the window.

<img width="57" height="51" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30af35dc-3f1c-4fa4-a547-9f9089c2131b" />

Your ship deals with 2 points of damage per shoot.

Some enemies can be split in two when they are destroyed. Those enemies are bigger than usual and their hp will be divided by 2.

<img width="219" height="192" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/091ac6bb-5dff-4208-b61d-1842f8e47b4f" />

You can pause the game by pressing the “escape” button and you will have the choice to continue or quit the game.

<img width="487" height="431" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/86be5f3f-28b6-4c85-9f3d-83cc1040b92c" />

If you die, a game over screen will appear and the game will exit by itself.

<img width="387" height="630" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/59536d38-4dbe-4b02-8072-a725642a4a3e" />

When you finish the game, a message saying that you win will appear on the window and you will be able to end the game.
